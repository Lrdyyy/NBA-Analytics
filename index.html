How these numbers are worked out

Defense: ESPN team opponent stats. Defensive efficiency = opponent points ÷ estimated possessions (FGA + 0.44 × FTA − OREB + TOV) × 100, shown as a %. Offense: efficiency, pace, eFG%, TS%, turnover % and assist ratio are NBA.com numbers via Hoops Nightly; shot volumes are from StatMuse and ESPN. Some teams' shot mix is estimated from their shooting percentages (flagged on their deep dive).

Matchup edge compares the attacking team's offense rank with the defending team's rank in the same area (1 = best on each side). The better rank gets the edge; within 5 spots is "even".

NBA Projected Game Score uses your formula with season-to-date numbers: a = Team 1 offensive efficiency × its possessions, x = Team 1 defensive efficiency × its possessions (same for Team 2 as b and y), then ((a + x) + (b + y)) ÷ 2.

Players cover the Lakers, Spurs, Knicks, 76ers, Warriors, Raptors and Pistons — each team's last 10 games (box scores from FantasyData, SI, ESPN, NBA.com and CBS; every game checked so player points add up to the final score). Minutes are rounded.
